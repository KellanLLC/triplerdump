Straight answers

About the 20 yard

Why is the 20 yard your most-rented bin?

Because it covers the most jobs for the least money. It is the size that handles a kitchen, a floor, or a yard without running out of room, and it still fits in a driveway. When a 15 feels tight, the 20 is $50 more.

Can I start with a 15 and switch to a 20?

Yes. If the bin fills mid-project we haul it and drop the next one in the same trip; that is the bin switch, $200. One of our reviews is exactly that story. Cheaper still is to start with the 20.

What is the price for a week?

$375 for 4 to 7 days, plus Utah sales tax at checkout. $350 for 1 to 3 days. Both include delivery, pickup and the dump fee.
