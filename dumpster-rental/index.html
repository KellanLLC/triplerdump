Straight answers

Dumpster sizes: questions

What size dumpster do I need?

Three sizes: 15 yard (about five pickup loads; garage cleanouts, small remodels, yard waste), 20 yard (about seven loads; kitchens, flooring, landscaping, estate cleanouts; our most-rented), and 25 yard (about nine loads; construction, roofing, whole-home cleanouts). Not sure? Call and describe the job; we will fit the bin to it.

How much does a dumpster rental cost?

One price per size for a rental of up to 7 days: 15 yard ${{TRD:d15_47}}, 20 yard ${{TRD:d20_47}}, 25 yard ${{TRD:d25_47}}. Delivery, pickup and the dump fee are included; Utah sales tax is added at checkout. Extra days are ${{TRD:ext_day}} each.

How much weight is included?

{{TRD:tons15}} tons in a 15 yard, {{TRD:tons20}} tons in a 20 yard and {{TRD:tons25}} tons in a 25 yard. Household and remodel debris rarely gets there; concrete, dirt, brick and shingles can. Over the allowance it is ${{TRD:over_ton}} a ton.

Which dumpster fits in a driveway?

The 15 and the 20 fit a standard driveway, about one parking space. The 25 needs a longer footprint: a long driveway, a job site or a lot. Tell us where it is going when you book and we will say whether it fits.
