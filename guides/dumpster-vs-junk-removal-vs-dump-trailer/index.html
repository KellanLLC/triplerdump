Rent a dumpster when you have a lot of debris and a few days to fill it, book junk removal when you want a crew to load it all in one visit, and rent a dump trailer when you want to haul and dump it yourself. We do all three, so here is how we would choose.

The short version

  • Lots of debris, several days of work: a roll-off dumpster.
  • A pile that is ready to go and you do not want to lift it: junk removal.
  • Small loads over time, and you are fine doing the driving: a dump trailer.
  • A job bigger than one bin: a dumpster plus a bin switch.

Roll-off dumpster: the most room for the money

A bin is one price for up to 7 days: ${{TRD:d15_47}} for a 15 yard, ${{TRD:d20_47}} for a 20 yard and ${{TRD:d25_47}} for a 25 yard, with delivery, pickup and the dump fee included up to the weight allowance. You load it at your own pace, a little each evening or all at once on Saturday.

It is the right call for remodels, roof tear-offs, basement and garage cleanouts that take more than a day, and anything that produces debris as you go. The trade-off: you do the loading, and the bin needs a spot in your driveway or on the job site for the week. See dumpster sizes and prices.

Junk removal: you do not lift anything

Our junk removal is ${{TRD:junk}} flat, weekends only. The crew shows up, loads everything, hauls it away and pays the dump. It covers one load.

It wins when the stuff is already sitting there: a move-out, an estate, a basement full of furniture, a garage you have been meaning to clear. It is also the answer when you cannot lift heavy items, have no room for a bin, or your HOA will not allow one. The limits are real, though: weekends only, one visit and one load. If the job will keep producing debris for days, a bin is the better fit. See junk removal in northern Utah.

Dump trailer: haul it yourself

The dump trailer is ${{TRD:trailer_day}} per day for 1 to 14 days, plus a ${{TRD:trailer_dep}} refundable deposit. You tow it, load it, take it to the dump and pay the dump fee there, then bring it back.

It suits people who want to do the hauling, have smaller loads spread over time, or need to move material more than once. Local gate fees are modest; Davis Landfill charges district residents $10 for a load of 10,000 pounds or less, as of September 2, 2026. The cost is your time and the driving, and every load has to be covered. Our dumpster cost guide has the local dump fees side by side. See dump trailer rental.

Bin switch for jobs bigger than one bin

On a big cleanout or a long remodel, one bin may not be enough. A bin switch swaps your full bin for an empty one in the same trip, so the work does not stop. The switch is ${{TRD:binswitch}} and the new bin is billed as a normal rental. Call as soon as you can see the top of the pile. See bin switch service.

Dumpster vs Bagster

A Bagster is a bag you buy, fill and schedule for collection. According to the Bagster site, it holds 3 cubic yards and up to 3,300 pounds, and no more than 1 cubic yard of that can be heavy material such as dirt, brick, rock, concrete, sod or stucco. Bagster specifications

For comparison, our smallest bin is 15 yards, about five times the volume of one bag. A bag makes sense for a small, light job when you have a spot it can sit in; check whether collection is available at your address and what it costs. For a remodel, a roof or a real cleanout, a roll-off holds far more and handles heavier debris. We do not know Bagster's local price, so compare it yourself against ours.

Quick answers

Is it cheaper to rent a dumpster or hire junk removal?

For a lot of debris, a dumpster is cheaper per load: our 20 yard is ${{TRD:d20_47}} for up to 7 days, against ${{TRD:junk}} for one junk removal load. Junk removal costs more because our crew does all the lifting.

Dumpster rental vs junk removal: which is better for a house cleanout?

If the house will take days to sort, a dumpster lets you work at your pace. If everything is sorted and ready to go, junk removal clears it in one weekend visit without you lifting anything.

Dumpster rental vs Bagster: which should I choose?

A Bagster holds 3 cubic yards and up to 3,300 pounds, with no more than 1 cubic yard of heavy debris, according to Bagster. Our smallest bin is 15 yards. For small, light jobs a bag can work; for remodels, roofing or cleanouts, a roll-off holds far more.

Can I rent a dump trailer instead of a dumpster?

Yes. Our dump trailer is ${{TRD:trailer_day}} per day plus a ${{TRD:trailer_dep}} refundable deposit. You tow, load and dump it yourself and pay the dump fee at the gate.

Is junk removal available on weekdays?

No, our junk removal runs on weekends only. If you need it gone on a weekday, a dumpster delivered same-day or next-day is the usual answer.
