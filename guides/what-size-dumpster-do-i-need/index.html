What size dumpster do I need?

Which dumpster size fits your project: bathroom, kitchen, garage, basement, house cleanout, flooring, deck, fence, roofing or yard waste. A Utah guide.

For most home projects a 15 yard handles a bathroom or a garage, a 20 yard handles a kitchen, flooring or an estate cleanout, and a 25 yard handles roofs, whole-house cleanouts and construction. Volume picks the size, but weight decides whether it works.

The three sizes in plain terms

  • 15 yard: about five pickup-truck loads, {{TRD:tons15}} tons included, ${{TRD:d15_47}}. The weekend bin. 15 yard dumpster rental
  • 20 yard: about seven pickup-truck loads, {{TRD:tons20}} tons included, ${{TRD:d20_47}}. The one we rent most, and the safe pick when you are unsure. 20 yard dumpster rental
  • 25 yard: about nine pickup-truck loads, {{TRD:tons25}} tons included, ${{TRD:d25_47}}. Roofing, whole houses, construction. 25 yard dumpster rental

The 15 and 20 sit in a standard driveway. The 25 needs a longer footprint, so tell us where it is going and we will say whether it fits. Every size is one price for up to 7 days with delivery, pickup and the dump fee included.

Remodels: bathrooms and kitchens

One bathroom is a 15 yard job: the vanity, toilet, tub or shower surround, drywall and flooring. Watch the tile. Tile set in a mortar bed, and cast-iron tubs, are heavy for their size, so a bathroom gut can use a fair share of the weight allowance without filling much of the bin.

A kitchen is a 20 yard job. Cabinets and boxes take up room fast, and if the flooring and some drywall come out at the same time, a 15 fills up early. Stone or tile countertops add weight; mention them when you book.

If you are doing a kitchen and a bathroom together, or a whole main floor, look at the 25 yard or plan on a bin switch partway through.

Cleanouts: garage, basement, whole house

A garage cleanout fits a 15 yard for most one- and two-car garages. If the garage is packed to the ceiling, or you are clearing the shed and the side yard too, step up to the 20.

A basement is usually a 15 or a 20, depending on how much furniture is down there. A basement finish, where the debris is mostly framing offcuts, drywall scraps and packaging, fits a 15 for one or two rooms and a 20 for a whole basement.

A whole house or estate cleanout is a 20 or a 25. Furniture is bulky but light, so these jobs run out of room before they run out of weight. For a large home, a 25 and a bin switch beats guessing low. Appliances with refrigerant, tires and electronics cannot go in the bin, mattresses need a call first, and paint, chemicals and other liquids cannot go in at all; see what can go in a dumpster.

If you would rather not do the loading yourself, our junk removal crew loads one full load for ${{TRD:junk}} on weekends.

Flooring, decks and fences

Carpet and pad are bulky. A few rooms fit a 15; a whole level of carpet, or carpet plus the tack strip and subfloor, is a 20. Tile flooring is the opposite: it takes little room but weighs a lot.

Deck removal is a 15 for a small deck and a 20 for a large one. Cut long boards and joists down so they lie flat; a bin full of crossed boards holds far less than it should.

Fence removal is a 15 or a 20 depending on length. Wood panels are light. The concrete around the posts is not, so if the footings are coming out too, call first.

Roof tear-offs: weight is the limit, not space

Shingles are the classic case of a bin that hits its weight allowance while it still looks half empty. Asphalt shingles are heavy, architectural shingles weigh more than 3-tab, and older homes around Ogden often have two or three layers on them.

So we do not size a roof by eye. Call us at (801) 564-3164 with the number of squares, how many layers are coming off and the shingle type. Your roofer or the original bid will have the square count, and the weight per square is printed on the bundle wrapper or the manufacturer's spec sheet. With those numbers we will tell you whether one bin covers it and which size, and the 25 yard is usually where a roof ends up.

Yard waste, concrete and dirt

Branches, brush and green waste are light and bulky: a 15 for a typical yard cleanup, a 20 for a full landscaping tear-out.

Sod, dirt, rock, concrete and brick are a different story. They can reach the weight allowance with very little of the bin used. Heavy material has weight limits, so call before you book and we will tell you how much can go in. Clean dirt and sod can also go to Davis Landfill in Layton as clean fill; see the Wasatch Integrated fee schedule.

When you are between two sizes

Go up one. The difference between a 15 yard at ${{TRD:d15_47}} and a 20 yard at ${{TRD:d20_47}} is less than a second delivery, and a bin loaded above the rim cannot be hauled until the extra comes off. If you would like a second opinion, call or text us and describe the job; we would rather size it right than sell you the big one. When you know the size, book your dumpster online.

Straight answers

Quick answers

What size dumpster do I need for a bathroom remodel?

A 15 yard for one bathroom. It holds about five pickup loads and includes {{TRD:tons15}} tons, which covers the vanity, toilet, tub, drywall and flooring. Mention heavy tile or a cast-iron tub when you book.

What size dumpster do I need for a garage cleanout?

A 15 yard for most one- and two-car garages. If it is packed to the ceiling or you are clearing a shed as well, take the 20 yard.

What size dumpster do I need for a house cleanout?

A 20 or a 25 yard. Furniture and household goods are bulky but light, so these jobs run out of room first. For a large home, a 25 yard plus a bin switch is the safer plan.

What size dumpster do I need for roof shingles?

It depends on the weight, not the volume. Call us with the number of squares, the number of layers and the shingle type, and we will tell you whether one bin covers it. Roofs usually go in a 25 yard.

How big is a 20 yard dumpster?

It holds about seven pickup-truck loads and includes {{TRD:tons20}} tons of debris. It fits a standard driveway; if you are unsure about your spot, tell us where it is going and we will tell you whether it fits.

What if I order a dumpster that is too small?

Call us. We can swap the full bin for an empty one in the same trip for ${{TRD:binswitch}}, and the new bin is billed as a normal rental. Do not load above the rim; an overfilled bin cannot be hauled.
