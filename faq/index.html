Straight answers

Before you book

What size dumpster do I need?

Three sizes: 15 yard (about five pickup loads; garage cleanouts, small remodels, yard waste), 20 yard (about seven loads; kitchens, flooring, landscaping, estate cleanouts; our most-rented), and 25 yard (about nine loads; construction, roofing, whole-home cleanouts). Not sure? Call and describe the job; we will fit the bin to it.

What does the price include?

Delivery, pickup, a rental of up to 7 days and the dump fee, in one price per size: 15 yard ${{TRD:d15_47}}, 20 yard ${{TRD:d20_47}}, 25 yard ${{TRD:d25_47}}. Utah sales tax is added at checkout. Extra days, weight over the allowance and prohibited items are spelled out on the terms page. {{TRD:fuel_note}}

Is the dump fee really included?

Yes, on every bin rental, up to a weight allowance: {{TRD:tons15}} tons in a 15 yard, {{TRD:tons20}} in a 20 and {{TRD:tons25}} in a 25. A normal household or remodel load stays under it. Very heavy material (concrete, dirt, brick) gets there fast, and weight over the allowance is ${{TRD:over_ton}} a ton, so call first for those.

How long can I keep the bin?

Up to 7 days for the one price. Done sooner? Call and we pick it up early. Need longer? Call before pickup and we extend it at ${{TRD:ext_day}} a day. If the bin fills before the job is done, a bin switch hauls the full one and drops a fresh one in the same trip.

How fast can you deliver?

Same-day and next-day delivery are available most of the year, depending on where the trucks are. Book online with your date or call and ask.

Where does the bin go?

Driveway, job site or lot, wherever you point. Drivers set it on boards if you want the concrete protected; just say so when you book. If the bin has to sit on a public street, rules vary by city, so check with your city's public works office first (Ogden requires an encroachment permit); a driveway placement avoids the question.

What can I not put in it?

Hazardous waste, wet paint and other liquids, asbestos, propane tanks and fuel, tires, appliances with refrigerant (Freon), and electronics such as TVs, monitors, computers and batteries. Each prohibited item found in a load is ${{TRD:prohibited}} under our terms. Mattresses need separate handling and an extra fee, so call first. The Weber County Transfer Station and Davis Landfill take tires, fridges, mattresses and electronics for a small fee; our guide to what can go in a dumpster has their prices.

How do I book and pay?

Pick the size and the dates on the booking page, enter the address and pay by card. You get a confirmation text with your reference number, a reminder the day before delivery and the day before pickup. Commercial accounts can request a quote instead and Joseph calls back.

Where do you deliver?

From our shop in West Haven to Weber, Davis, Box Elder, Cache, Morgan and Salt Lake County, and Utah County as far south as Springville. Brigham City is about 25 minutes away, Tremonton about 40, Cottonwood Heights about 45, Logan about 55, and Springville, the far end, about an hour and a quarter; book that end a few days ahead. The service area page lists every city with how far it is from us.

Do you do junk removal?

Yes, weekends only, ${{TRD:junk}} flat: we bring the truck and the crew, load it, haul it and pay the dump. Weekdays, a 15 yard bin is usually the answer.

Can I rent a dump trailer instead?

Yes. ${{TRD:trailer_day}} a day, 1 to 14 days, with a ${{TRD:trailer_dep}} refundable deposit. You do the hauling and the dump runs yourself; it is the do-it-yourself option.

Who am I dealing with?

Joseph Rodrigues and his family. Triple R Dump is family-owned and operated out of West Haven, and the person who answers the phone is the person who books the job.
