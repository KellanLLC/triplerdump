Straight answers

Dumpster rental in Roy: questions

Can I rent a dumpster in Roy for just a weekend?

Yes. One price covers anything from one day to seven, so book it for Friday and call when you are done; we pick it up early at no extra cost. Roy is five minutes from the shop, so an early pickup is easy to fit in.

Do you do junk removal in Roy?

Yes, on weekends, ${{TRD:junk}} flat: we bring the truck and the crew, load it, haul it away and pay the dump. On a weekday, a 15 yard bin in the driveway is usually the cheaper way to clear the same pile.

Does Roy have free dumpster days?

Roy City puts free dumpsters out at its Public Works yard during set weeks of the year, for Roy residents only with proof of residency; check royutah.gov or call Roy Public Works at (801) 774-1090 for this year's dates. Those programs are for household and yard items on the city's dates. A rental is the answer if you missed them, need more room than a shared bin, need it for more than a day, or have remodeling, roofing or contractor debris.
