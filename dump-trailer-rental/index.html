How it works

Book the days online, pay the rental plus the refundable deposit, and pick the trailer up from us in West Haven. Haul whatever you need, dump it yourself, bring it back clean on the last day. The deposit comes back to your card once it is checked in.

Straight answers

Questions people ask

What is the deposit for?

It is $300, refundable, and it covers the trailer coming back on time and in one piece. It is returned to your card once the trailer is checked in.

Can I keep it longer than I booked?

Call before the last day and we extend it at the daily rate if it is not booked behind you. Bringing it back late without a call runs into the late fee on the terms page, so call.

Is the dump fee included?

No. With the trailer you are doing your own dump runs and paying the dump directly. The bins include the dump fee; the trailer is the do-it-yourself option, which is why it is cheaper per day.
